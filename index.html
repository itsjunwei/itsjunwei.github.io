Research

I work on 3-D sound event localization and detection (SELD): systems that jointly determine what sound events occur, in which direction they occur, and at what distance. Within that, I work on four things: robustness when real-world class distributions are long-tailed, geometric formulations of the regression target, lightweight models for edge and hearable deployment, and label-efficient training.

Thesis: Advancing Environmental Acoustic Intelligence: Robust, Geometric, and Lightweight Frameworks for 3-D Sound Event Localization and Detection

Demo: presented a live polyphonic SELD system at ICASSP 2024, Seoul.
